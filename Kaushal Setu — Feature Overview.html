Module 00 — About

Kaushal Setu

A living register of what happens after training ends.

Government skilling programmes track enrolment and certification well, but lose sight of trainees the moment they leave the classroom. Kaushal Setu closes that gap — a consent-based system that follows trainees into employment, self-employment or the next attempt, and turns that trail into evidence the department can act on.

Module 01

Consent-Based Trainee Profile

One record, owned by the trainee.

Every trainee gets a single digital profile linking their course, provider and certificate — created only with explicit, revocable consent. No data is pulled or shared without the trainee knowingly opting in first.

  • ＋OTP-verified identity, one unified registration number per trainee
  • ＋Plain-language consent screen shown before any tracking begins
  • ＋Consent can be withdrawn anytime without affecting certification
Relevant building blocks
Aadhaar-linked ID (opt-in)OTP authConsent ledger
Module 02

Job & Placement Linking

Connecting the classroom to the workplace.

Training records are linked with placement and employment signals from job portals and employer databases, so a placement isn't just self-reported — it's cross-referenced against real hiring data where available.

  • ＋Integrates with National Career Service and partner job portals
  • ＋Captures employer, role, salary and joining date at the point of placement
  • ＋Flags mismatches between claimed and verified placement records
Relevant building blocks
NCS APIEmployer registryREST integration
Module 03

Automated + Assisted Follow-ups

Low-burden check-ins that don't rely on luck.

Scheduled SMS, WhatsApp and IVR follow-ups go out at 3, 6, 12 and 24 months. If a trainee doesn't respond, the case escalates to a human counsellor instead of the record going cold.

  • ＋Multi-channel reminders: SMS, WhatsApp, voice call
  • ＋Non-response auto-escalates to assisted (human) follow-up
  • ＋One-tap update links — no app download needed
Relevant building blocks
Twilio / WhatsApp APIIVREscalation queue
Module 04

Self-Employment & Apprenticeship Tracking

Not every good outcome is a payslip.

Trainees who start a business or take up an apprenticeship get a dedicated tracking path — capturing business type, income and continuity — instead of being mis-logged as "unemployed".

  • ＋Separate outcome forms for business owners and apprentices
  • ＋Tracks whether the venture is still active over time
  • ＋Feeds into the same wage-progression analytics as formal jobs
Relevant building blocks
Business income logApprenticeship registry
Module 05

Employer Verification

Trust, but verify.

A lightweight employer-side confirmation step — OTP or portal-based — cross-checks trainee-reported placements, reducing false placement claims that distort provider performance data.

  • ＋Employer confirms placement via a simple OTP/portal flow
  • ＋Discrepancies between trainee and employer data are flagged
  • ＋Protects genuine training providers from inflated-claim competitors
Relevant building blocks
Employer portalOTP confirmation
Module 06

Wage & Retention Progression

A job on day one isn't the whole story.

Salary and job-retention are tracked longitudinally at 3, 6, 12 and 24 months, revealing whether training leads to durable, growing careers — not just a first placement.

  • ＋Median wage tracked per cohort over 24 months
  • ＋Retention rate — still employed vs. dropped out
  • ＋Early-warning signal when a course's wage curve flattens
Relevant building blocks
Time-series analyticsCohort tracking
Module 07

Cohort, Course, Provider & District Analytics

The dashboard the department actually needed.

One dashboard rolls outcome data up by course, training provider, district and demographic group — so officials can compare, defend budgets, and target underperforming programmes.

  • ＋Provider leaderboard by verified placement rate
  • ＋District-level heatmaps for regional targeting
  • ＋Demographic breakdowns to catch equity gaps
Relevant building blocks
Power BI / MetabasePostgreSQL analytics layer
Module 08

Skill-Gap & Attrition Detection

Find out why, not just what.

When placement or retention drops for a course or cohort, the system auto-flags likely causes — market mismatch, low wages, employer shortage — from patterns in the outcome data.

  • ＋Auto-flags courses with low market demand or high attrition
  • ＋Surfaces the most common trainee-reported non-placement reasons
  • ＋Turns raw non-placement data into a remedial-action list
Relevant building blocks
Rule-based flagsPattern detection
Module 09

Privacy & Data Protection

Built to earn trust, not just collect data.

Every layer of the system — consent, storage, access — is designed around the principle that outcome data is sensitive personal information, not a KPI to be extracted at any cost.

  • ＋End-to-end encryption of personal and income data
  • ＋Role-based access — officials see only what their role permits
  • ＋Full audit trail of who accessed what, and when
